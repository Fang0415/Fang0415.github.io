# Interdisciplinary card expansion prompt

Use case: stylized-concept

Asset type: a production-ready contact sheet of twelve square editorial cards, arranged as an exact 4 columns by 3 rows grid with equal square cells, perfectly aligned edges, no gutters, no borders, and no text.

Create twelve distinct thumbnail-readable concept illustrations in this reading order:

1. Saturn — a ringed planet held in a simple orbital gesture.
2. Mars — a cratered planet with one sweeping exploration path.
3. Solar system — a simplified hand-cranked orrery with a central sun and only a few orbiting planets.
4. Computer science — a compact terminal-like rectangle connected to three network nodes, with no letters or numbers.
5. Biology — one sprouting leaf whose vein becomes a simplified DNA-like double curve.
6. Physics — a pendulum whose arc becomes a planetary orbit.
7. Chemistry — one laboratory flask whose rising bubbles become a molecular bond pattern.
8. Literature — an open book whose single flowing line becomes a story path.
9. Philosophy — a human profile facing a small imperfect maze with one open exit.
10. History — a classical column connected to a simple three-point timeline, with no dates or numerals.
11. Mathematics — a hand-drawn compass tracing one imperfect geometric circle with a few abstract calculation marks and no readable text.
12. Astronomy — a small telescope aimed at one oversized star inside an irregular night-sky carrier.

Every tile uses one full-bleed opaque color from the verified Anthropic palette, covering every corner: cactus `#BCD1CA`, heather `#CBCADB`, oat `#E3DACC`, clay `#D97757`, olive `#788C5D`, sky `#6A9BCC`, fig `#C46686`, or coral `#EBCECE`.

Use the Anthropic editorial illustration language: near-black `#141413` naive ink gestures with thick, slightly uneven rounded strokes; one irregular ivory `#FAF9F5` carrier shape; simplified objects; deliberate asymmetry; flat two-dimensional forms; generous breathing room; one focal cluster occupying roughly 65–78% of each tile; readable at 96px.

Keep every tile opaque and visually independent after exact grid cropping. Use no transparency, white or black outer canvas, gutters, rounded outer corners, text, labels, logos, watermarks, photorealism, 3D, glossy lighting, technical line art, dense detail, or stock-vector polish.
